1. Overview & Data Controller

JobLatch AI ("JobLatch", "we", "us", or "our") operates an automated AI voice dispatch platform for trade contractor businesses ("Customers"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, process, store, and share information when trade contractors and their end-user callers ("Callers") interact with our voice dispatch service, website, and related applications.

2. Information We Collect

  • Inbound Caller Audio & Transcripts: Audio recordings, machine transcripts, timecodes, caller phone numbers, and caller-provided details (name, service address, description of electrical/HVAC/plumbing issue) captured during dispatch calls.
  • Contractor Account Data: Shop name, trade licenses, technician names, phone numbers, truck assignments, dispatch schedules, and administrative email addresses.
  • Payment Information: Pre-authorization deposit holds and fee processing details. Card details are processed directly by our payment processor (Stripe) via secure SMS payment links. JobLatch does not collect or store raw card numbers.
  • Technical Telemetry: IP addresses, browser user agents, call routing logs via telephony providers (Twilio), and diagnostic event metrics.

3. AI Disclosure & Call Recording Consent

JobLatch AI provides automated conversational voice technology. At the start of an inbound call, the automated assistant identifies itself and notifies the caller that the call may be recorded for dispatch and quality assurance purposes.

Customer Recording Compliance Obligation [TODO: legal review]: Trade contractor customers are solely responsible for ensuring compliance with applicable federal, state, and local wiretapping, eavesdropping, and two-party/all-party call recording consent statutes in every jurisdiction where they operate.

4. How We Use Information

We process collected data to:

  • Triage and route emergency trade service requests to customer-designated on-call technicians.
  • Synchronize work orders with Customer Field Service Management (FSM) systems (e.g., ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, Jobber).
  • Send automated customer dispatch confirmation SMS messages and technician tracking links.
  • Facilitate pre-authorization deposit holds via Stripe to protect contractor fuel and dispatch time.
  • Maintain operational reliability, detect fraud, and provide customer support.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

We partner with vetted infrastructure providers to deliver the dispatch engine:

  • Telephony & Voice Carriers (e.g., Twilio): Inbound SIP trunking, call routing, and SMS messaging.
  • AI & Speech Infrastructure (e.g., Google Cloud Gemini): Speech recognition, natural language emergency triage, and text-to-speech synthesis.
  • Payment Processing (Stripe, Inc.): PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment processing and card pre-authorization hold management.
